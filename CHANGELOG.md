## [0.6.0](https://github.com/Dirnei/gaudiballz/compare/v0.5.2...v0.6.0) (2026-09-16)


### Features

* Every level has its own leaderboard, shown right in the solved dialog
* Move around the board with the keyboard
* A redesigned home page with community stats and your progress
* The game has its flask logo as its app icon

* Win rate now counts restarts, so it means clears over tries
